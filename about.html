ABOUT ME

Ibrahim Feknous

BS/MS Electrical Engineering
New Jersey Institute of Technology

Hi, I’m Ibrahim. I like having something to build and a reason to figure out how it works.

A lot of my time lately has gone into the solar car. I enjoy the car itself, but a big part of why I’ve stuck with it is the people. It’s exciting to have a group of people working toward something that none of us could build alone, especially when an idea finally starts to take shape.

I’m drawn to projects that give me an excuse to explore something new. That’s led me into autonomous racing and, more recently, a weather balloon with custom electronics. I want to learn more about space and remote sensing, and building something we can actually send up feels like a good place to start.

Electronics is where a lot of these interests meet. Right now, I’m spending time on a motor controller and learning more about how electric vehicles work. I like being able to follow a question far enough to try an idea myself, even when it takes a few attempts to get there.

I also enjoy helping other people get started on their own ideas. That’s a big reason I’m involved in IEEE and MakeNJIT. I want more people to have a place to try something they’re curious about, ask questions, and find others to build with.
