ABOUT ME

Ibrahim Feknous

BS/MS Electrical Engineering
New Jersey Institute of Technology

I’m Ibrahim, a BS/MS electrical engineering student at NJIT focused on power electronics and embedded hardware.

I like working close to the hardware. Designing a PCB is one part of it; writing code, checking measurements, and figuring out why a circuit behaves differently than expected are just as interesting to me.

I’ve worked on electronics for autonomous construction robots, solar PV and energy storage projects, and autonomous vehicle research in the NJIT UNICS Lab. These experiences have helped me develop my interests in power conversion, motor control, and how hardware and software work together.

At NJIT, I lead the Solar Car Team and help organize technical projects and events through IEEE. I enjoy working with other students, sharing what I’ve learned, and helping teams get their ideas built. Teaching FED 101 and tutoring through IEEE-HKN have also given me more opportunities to work with students on circuits, programming, and electronics.

Right now, I’m developing a regenerative STM32 motor controller and a weather balloon payload with custom electronics. I’m looking to keep building my experience in PCB design, power electronics, and embedded systems through projects and engineering opportunities.
