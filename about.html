ABOUT ME

Ibrahim Feknous

BS/MS Electrical Engineering
New Jersey Institute of Technology

Hi, I’m Ibrahim! I like making my imagination a reality.

I spend a lot of my time with the Solar Car Team at NJIT. I enjoy building the car, but the people make the experience mean so much to me. There’s something exciting about a group working toward an idea that none of us could bring to life alone. Seeing it start to take shape is especially rewarding.

I’m drawn to projects that let me explore new ideas and tackle topics I haven’t figured out yet. One of my favorite parts of undergrad is having the chance to try so many things within electrical engineering. Each project gives me a better sense of what I enjoy and what I want to pursue next.

Lately, that curiosity has taken me into motor control, autonomous vehicles, and a high-altitude weather balloon. I like that there’s always another question to ask, or another idea I can try building.

I believe there’s always room to grow. I want to keep learning throughout my career, whether that comes from a class, someone I’m working with, or a project that makes me rethink my approach.
